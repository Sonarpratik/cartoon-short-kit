# 6. A technical point: Blender vs. Three.js

This matters if Claude is producing most of your scenes through code.

Blender — Asset and visual-quality pipeline

Build detailed models, edit materials, generate vegetation, create complex smoke and particle effects, and render cinematic shots.

Three.js — Real-time 3D pipeline

Useful for interactive staging, animation previews, procedural geometry, cameras, and real-time rendering with appropriate shaders and effects.

Remotion — Sequencing and finishing

Assemble shots, synchronize narration, manage timing, integrate overlays and effects, and export the final video.

Three.js can produce excellent visuals, but if Claude is creating scenes primarily from basic procedural boxes, cylinders, spheres, flat colors, and simple lines, the result will continue looking basic regardless of export resolution.

A stronger production pipeline uses reusable detailed assets, procedural material systems, specialized VFX, and cinematic lighting rigs rather than reconstructing everything from simple geometry for every shot.
