# solution: the real vignette is a light pool in the scene

**What:** the reference's night "vignette" is an elliptical pool of cool light centred on the action (walls get
a soft slash; outside falls to deep navy) — made by LIGHT, not by a post overlay. Verifiers flag post-vignettes
as "cheap filter" the moment they pass ~0.86 corner/centre.
**Why it works:** a pool directs the eye like a stage spot and keeps corners alive (dark but coloured).
**Pattern:** post vig stays at the measured corner 0.86; mood vignetting comes from a wide spot/gradient pool +
one warm counter-pool (window/lantern). Source: burglar ep s001 study (railway_notes.md).
