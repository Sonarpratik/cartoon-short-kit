# Inked gates misread the no-outline look
The inked-style thresholds scored the CB-approved look 3/7 and 4/7. Gates must be measured on frames in the same style: use density_check.py --style 3d. Also: fine weave (burlap, tarp) aliases to a checker at distance, wood grain bands must run along the plank, f/2.8 kills edges, lamps need a clamped emissive plus bloom threshold or they go white.
