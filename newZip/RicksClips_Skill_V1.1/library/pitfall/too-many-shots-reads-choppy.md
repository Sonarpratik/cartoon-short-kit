# pitfall: one dissolve every ~4 s reads as "too many cuts" — HIGH (CB on ONODA, 2026-11-05)

**Symptom:** CB on ONODA: "i wish it had less cuts". The cut had 0 hard cuts but 8 shots / 18 framings / 7 dissolves
in 32.8 s; CB counts every dissolve as a cut.
**Fix (standing rule, SKILL.md):** travel the camera between framings INSIDE a shot (glides, aim.ts stations, camAt
legs) and hard-cut or dissolve only on a real location / time jump. Target about 3-4 shots per 30 s. Stage the story so
consecutive beats share a set (e.g. the leaflets, the three fakes and the campfire could all be one travelling jungle shot).
**Detect:** count shot changes in the shot table before building: > 1 per 8 s = rework the staging.
**Priority:** HIGH.
