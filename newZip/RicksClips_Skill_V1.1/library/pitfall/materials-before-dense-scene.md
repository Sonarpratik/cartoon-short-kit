# pitfall: skipping the finished toon stage (CB 2026-11-09) — HIGH
The market street test went straight to materials and came out flat. A later test used plain colours as the first stage, which was also wrong. The approved airfield had two full stages: a complete Python build finished in the toon look and polished in rounds (Stage A), then the same scene converted to materials and engine light (Stage B).
**Fix:** finish Stage A and pass the gates before any Stage B work.
**Detect:** an episode with no Stage A page of rounds, scores and grids.
