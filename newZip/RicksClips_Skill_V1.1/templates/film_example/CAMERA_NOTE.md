## Director's note
The mainspring: the camera winds one way round the station master while the trouble builds, lets go on "You see" and runs back along the same path to the frame it started on.
- Hook, "stopped": a push from whole body to a knee-up medium that lands as he presses the watch.
- Turn, "see": the camera pushes past the laughing kid to a low medium, his raised finger against the sky beside the giant clock.
- Payoff, "again": the unwind, a sweep with 4 degrees of roll past the laugh spot (the kid now starstruck on "one") that settles on frame 0 (`"loop": true` on the last station).
One WARN kept: the whole body fits in 57 % of face frames (target 65). It comes from three mediums: the hook, the turn, and "told", which breaks a run of whole-body rests.
