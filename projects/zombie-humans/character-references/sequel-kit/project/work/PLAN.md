## World concept
Same compound as Part 2's #1 pick (police station + school behind one steel gate). Twenty days pass inside it, then the threat comes from the road.
- **Set-piece:** the steel gate at night: Troy knocks alone, then returns in the headlights of the raider trucks.
- **Locations (6):** L1 Compound yard (day / dusk / night) · L2 Storeroom (school gym store: shelves of rice, cans, water, medicine) · L3 Classroom dorm (cots, chalkboard day tally, window) · L4 Gate + road outside (night, headlights) · L5 Raider camp (garage, oil-drum fire, map table) · L6 Watchtower at night.
- **Scale ladder:** aerial compound wides → group mids → macros (padlock, cans, Troy's eyes, map with red X, empty cot).
- **Light arc:** bright day (Day 1) → golden afternoon (Day 10) → warm campfire dusk (safe) → cold night (Day 20 knock) → grey dawn (he's gone) → black → headlight white → fire-orange raider camp → moonlit watch → campfire with doubt.
